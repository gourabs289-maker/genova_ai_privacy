Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Genova AI ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Genova AI mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our core features, the App requires certain permissions and collects specific types of data:

  • Photos and Images: The App requires access to your device's photo gallery to select reference images. These images are used strictly for the purpose of generating your requested portrait.
  • Anonymous Device Identifiers: We utilize anonymous authentication to provide a seamless experience without requiring an email or password. This generates a unique, anonymous identifier tied to your device to manage your digital wallet and points balance.
  • In-App Purchases: If you purchase points, the transaction is processed securely through Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card, UPI, or banking information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To process your selected images and generate the requested portraits.
  • To maintain and update your virtual points balance securely.
  • To improve the performance, safety, and functionality of the App.

3. Data Storage and Security

Image Processing: Reference photos uploaded to the App are processed securely. Once the final portrait is generated and delivered to your screen, the reference data is not retained or used for any other purpose.

Local Saving: Generated portraits are only saved to your device's local gallery when you explicitly click the "Save" button.

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your anonymous identifiers and ensure that your in-app point balances remain secure.

4. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, primarily for app analytics, secure authentication, and payment processing. These services include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics / Firebase

These third-party providers have their own Privacy Policies addressing how they use such information.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete this from our servers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
